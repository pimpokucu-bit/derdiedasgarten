📖 How to Play
1
📚 Take the Quiz
You'll see a German noun. Choose the correct article: der, die, or das.
⌨️
Keyboard Shortcuts
Press M for der, F for die, N for das, Enter to go next.
2
⭐ Earn Points
Each correct answer gives you 10 points. Keep a streak for bonus points!
3
🎯 Complete Daily Targets
Answer 10 questions, get 7 correct, and reach 3 streak daily for extra points!
4
🛍️ Buy Flowers
Spend your points at the Flower Store to buy beautiful flowers.
5
🌱 Plant Your Garden
Click an empty plot in your garden and choose a flower from your collection to plant it.
6
🏆 Unlock Achievements
Each achievement gives you bonus points! Claim them in the Achievements page.
7
✏️ Name Your Garden
Click the name board on top of your garden to give it a custom name!
🌟 The more you learn, the more your garden grows!
